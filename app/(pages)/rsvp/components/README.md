# RSVP

## Questions

1. General Attendance
       •    Will you be attending our wedding? (Yes / No)
       •    How many people in your party will be attending?

2. Travel & Accommodation
       •    When do you plan to arrive in Honolulu? (Date selection or open text field)
       •    Where will you be staying during your trip? (Hotel, Airbnb, with family, etc.)
       •    Will you have a rental car while in Honolulu? (Yes / No)

   Out of Town Guests Section:

   - Will you and your wedding party be joing the provided bus transportation to/from the wedding venue?
   - Please provide the best phone number?
   - Out of town guests: Where will you be staying? Please specific as possible.
   - Are you planning on renting a car?

3. Wedding Day Logistics
       •    Do you have any dietary restrictions or allergies we should be aware of? (Open text field)
       •    Will you be joining the provided transportation to the wedding venue? (Yes / No)
       •    (If yes) How many people in your party will be using the transportation?

4. Wedding Week Events (Customize based on your planned events!)
       •    Will you be attending the [Rehearsal Dinner]? (Yes / No)
       •    Will you be attending the [Welcome Party]? (Yes / No)
       •    Will you be joining us for a [Beach Day]? (Yes / No)
       •    Will you be attending the [Post-Wedding Brunch]? (Yes / No)
       •    (If yes) How many people in your party will be attending?

5. Special Requests & Guest Preferences
       •    Would you like to leave a note or message for the couple? (Optional open text field)

6. Contact Info (Optional but Helpful)
       •    What’s the best email and/or phone number to reach you for any last-minute updates?
